# Backup & Restore Center

V-Train is super protective of your privacy! We use a **"Zero-Server Architecture,"** which means all your watch history, settings, and private video bookmarks only exist on your computer. Nothing is ever uploaded to the cloud.
But because of this, if your computer breaks or you reinstall your browser, your data will be lost. Therefore, **backing up regularly is extremely important!**

> ![Backup & Restore Center](/assets/manual/backup_center_main.png)

### How to Backup (Export Data)
Open the Control Panel and click "📥 Export JSON" at the bottom.
The system will kindly pack everything up into a single `ZIP` file and download it straight to your computer.
* **Super Chunked Packing Tech**: If you're a super collector with thousands of high-quality screenshots, don't worry about your computer crashing during backup! V-Train has built-in "Smart Chunked Packing Tech" that automatically chops lots of images into tiny pieces to pack, ensuring a super smooth backup process.

### How to Restore (Import Data)
Got a new computer? Open the Control Panel, click "📤 Import JSON," and the system will take you to the Restore Center page.
Just drag the `ZIP` file you downloaded before, drop it onto the screen, and let go of your mouse... Ta-da! All your settings, hard work, and beautiful screenshot covers will come back in a second!
