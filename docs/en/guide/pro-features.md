# 6. Pro Features

Free users can store up to 200 watch history records (when the limit is reached, the oldest records will be automatically replaced). Upgrade to **V-Train Pro** to enjoy unlimited storage space and the following exclusive features:
* Unlimited watch history and bookmark storage
* Unlock the complete Bookmark Vault management system
* Enable the floating video interaction panel (Like, Snapshot, Quick Bookmark)
* Privacy Passcode Lock

### How to enable Pro?
1. Click **"⚡ Unlock V-Train Pro"** at the bottom of the dashboard.
2. Paste the license key you received upon purchase.
3. After successful verification, the panel will display "V-Train Pro Enabled", and all features will be unlocked!

### Forgot Passcode? (Rescue Unlock)
If you forget your privacy passcode, click **"Forgot Passcode?"** on the lock screen.
> ![Passcode Rescue](/assets/manual/passcode_rescue.png)
Enter your Pro license key for verification. Upon success, the passcode will be reset and cleared. It is highly recommended to perform a full backup immediately after unlocking!
