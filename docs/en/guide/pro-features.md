# Pro Advanced Features

The free version is already great, but if you're a hardcore video lover, upgrading to **V-Train Pro** will make you love it even more!

### The Small Limit of the Free Version (200 Records)
The free version of V-Train can only remember your watch progress for up to 200 videos.
Don't worry about running out of space! The system has a clever "Garbage Collection" mechanism. When you watch your 201st video, it quietly deletes the oldest record, always keeping the newest 200.

### The 3 Superpowers of Upgrading to Pro!
Once you become a Pro player, you unlock these awesome features:
1. **Unlimited Storage**: Say goodbye to the 200 limit! Watch as much as you want, save as much as you want, and the Garbage Collection mechanism will take a permanent vacation!
2. **Unlock the Beautiful Vault & Panel**: You finally get to use the "floating panel" in the bottom right corner of videos! Go crazy hitting the heart to save videos, take high-quality cover screenshots, and stash them all in your full-screen "Bookmark Vault" where you can sort them into folders!
3. **Top-Tier Privacy Password Lock**: You can set up a personal password. From then on, anyone who wants to open V-Train to see your watch history will have to get past your password first.

### How to Enable Pro?
1. At the very bottom of the Control Panel, click **"⚡ Unlock V-Train Pro"**.
2. Paste the License Key you purchased.
3. The system will instantly connect and verify it. Once successful, all features unlock in a flash!

### Forgot Your Password? (Password Rescue)
Oops, forgot the password you set? Don't panic!
On the password entry screen, click **"Forgot Passcode?"**.
> ![Password Rescue](/assets/manual/passcode_rescue.png)
Just enter your Pro License Key. Once the system confirms it's you, it will reset your password. We recommend making a data backup right after unlocking!
