# Bookmark Vault & Hover Panel

When you upgrade to Pro, or while watching a video, you'll find some handy little tools appear in the corner of your screen!

### Video Hover Panel
When you move your mouse over a video, a cute, semi-transparent panel will float up in the bottom right corner.
> ![Floating Interaction Panel](/assets/manual/floating_interaction_panel.png)
* **👍 Like / 😤 Dislike**: Click to give the video a rating! After rating, when you go back to the homepage and look at the thumbnail, a little badge will appear to remind you if it was good or bad.
* **❤️ Bookmark**: See a video you like? Click the heart to instantly save it to your "Bookmark Vault," and you can even pick which folder to put it in!
* **📸 Snapshot**: Sometimes the website's video cover doesn't look great. No problem! When you see an amazing scene, click this camera button. The system will take a high-quality "snapshot" of the screen and turn it into your very own custom bookmark cover!
* **📚 Open Vault**: Click this book to jump straight into your personal video library.

### Your Personal Bookmark Vault
This is a beautiful, full-screen video management center!
> ![Bookmark Vault Main](/assets/manual/bookmark_vault_main.png)
* **Unlimited Folders**: You can create tons of folders, and even put folders inside folders (just like on your computer). Right-click a folder to rename or delete it.
* **Smart Anti-Hotlink Tech (Dynamic CDN Learning)**: Ever saved a video only to find the cover image missing a few days later? Don't worry! V-Train has powerful anti-hotlink learning technology. It remembers where the image came from and automatically bypasses restrictions to ensure your covers are always visible!
* **Move Bookmarks Easily**: Hover your mouse over a video card, and you can give the video an easy-to-remember name or move it to another folder. Click the card to instantly fly back to the video's web page!
