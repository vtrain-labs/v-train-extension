# 4. Bookmark Vault & Interaction

### Floating Interaction Panel
While watching a video, a semi-transparent floating panel will appear in the bottom right corner.
> ![Floating Interaction Panel](/assets/manual/floating_interaction_panel.png)
* **👍 Like / 😤 Dislike**: Quickly rate the video. The rating will be displayed directly on the thumbnail on the homepage.
* **❤️ Bookmark**: Add the video to the bookmark vault, and instantly choose which folder to place it in.
* **📸 Snapshot Cover**: If you don't like the system's auto-captured cover, click this button at an exciting moment during playback. The system will use native Canvas technology to losslessly capture the current frame as a custom cover!

### Local Bookmark Vault
> ![Bookmark Vault Main](/assets/manual/bookmark_vault_main.png)
* **Folder Management**: Supports infinite levels of subfolders. Right-click a folder to "Rename", "Move", or "Delete".
* **Bookmark Actions**: Hover over a bookmark card to change the title, move it to another folder, or click the card to jump directly back to the playback page.
