# Dashboard Overview

The V-Train Dashboard is like your "Command Center." All your switches and settings are right here!

> ![Dashboard Main](/assets/manual/dashboard_main.png)

### Status & Switches
* **Stealth Mode**:
  > ![Monitoring Toggle](/assets/manual/toggle_monitoring.png)
  If you want to watch videos quietly today without leaving any history, click the switch in the top right to enter "Stealth Mode." All progress bars will be temporarily hidden!
* **Hover Panel Toggle**: When watching a video, a little panel pops out in the bottom right corner (for liking, screenshotting, and bookmarking). If you find it annoying, you can turn it off here.
* **Monitor Panel**: Want to know what video ID the system has caught or if it's currently recording? Turn on this little black window, and it will appear in the bottom left of your screen.
* **Progress Bar Color**: Getting bored of the default red progress bar? Click the color square to change it to your favorite custom color!
* **Language**: Don't understand the text? There's a language menu at the very bottom where you can switch between English and Chinese at any time!

### Shortcuts & Privacy Settings
* **Trained Sites (Rule Management)**:
  > ![Rule Management Button](/assets/manual/btn_rule_mgmt.png)
  The sites you've trained yourself or the "Rule Codes (VT-RULE)" you got from friends are managed here. You can delete ones you don't need or copy the code to share with others.
* **Bookmark Vault**: `[Pro Only]` Clicking this opens a beautiful, full-screen bookshelf to manage all your saved videos!
* **Password Lock / Clear Data**:
  > ![Privacy Settings](/assets/manual/btn_privacy_settings.png)
  Want absolute privacy? You can set up a "Password Lock." Once set, you'll need to enter the password every time you open this command center! If you want to wipe all your watch history in one go, just click "Clear Data."
