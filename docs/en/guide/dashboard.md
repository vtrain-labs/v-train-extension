# 2. Dashboard Overview

The V-Train dashboard is the core hub for managing all your settings.

> ![Dashboard Main](/assets/manual/dashboard_main.png)

### Status Monitoring Area
* **Monitoring / Stealth (Manual Pause)**:
  > ![Toggle Monitoring](/assets/manual/toggle_monitoring.png)
  You can pause V-Train's tracking at any time using this toggle. Enabling "Auto pause on browser close" protects your privacy when you leave.
* **Enable Quick Interaction Button**: Controls whether the floating interaction panel (Like/Bookmark/Snapshot) is shown in the bottom right corner of the video screen.
* **Show Monitor Panel**: Determines whether the V-Train system status black window is displayed in the bottom left corner of the webpage.
* **Progress Bar Color**: Click the color block to freely change your preferred progress bar color (default is red).

### Shortcuts and Management
* **Trained Sites (Rule Management)**:
  > ![Rule Management Button](/assets/manual/btn_rule_mgmt.png)
  Manage custom or imported site adaptation rules. You can delete unnecessary rules here or "Copy SN" to share rules with others.
* **Bookmark Vault**: `[Pro Only]` Access the full-screen bookmark management center.
* **Set Passcode / Clear Data**:
  > ![Privacy Settings](/assets/manual/btn_privacy_settings.png)
  Set an exclusive privacy passcode lock. Every time you open the dashboard, the passcode will be required to view data. Click "Clear Data" to one-click delete all local watch history and bookmarks (does not affect Pro status and rules).
