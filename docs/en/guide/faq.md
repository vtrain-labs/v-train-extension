# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Running into weird issues? Check here first for answers!

**Q: Why isn't the red progress bar showing up on the thumbnail?**
A: First, please open the Control Panel and make sure you've clicked "Authorize & Enable Current Site." If you've authorized it and it's still not showing, the site might be unknown to V-Train. Hurry and use the "Adaptive Engine" to train it manually!

**Q: Why does the "Snapshot" button sometimes show a red ❌?**
A: This usually happens because the website hides the video deep inside a protective shield (iframe) or has very strict security rules (CORS / CSP). V-Train actually has a very strong "penetration tech" built-in to try and grab the screenshot, but if the site's defenses are just too strong, it will unfortunately hit a wall.

**Q: If I accidentally delete the extension, is my data still there?**
A: **No, it's not!** To absolutely protect your privacy, V-Train never secretly uploads your data to the cloud. So once you remove the extension, the browser wipes all the records clean. **Always remember to regularly go to the "Backup & Restore Center" and download your backup file (ZIP) to stay safe!**

**Q: Will the free version's auto-delete (GC) delete the videos I saved?**
A: Absolutely not! If you've already added the video to your "Bookmark Vault," the system puts a protective charm on it, and the cleaning mechanism will never touch it.
